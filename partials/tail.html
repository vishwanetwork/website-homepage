How is Vishwa different from an API gateway?

Vishwa governs whether an agent may act, under which mandate, and within which limits—before routing execution.

What does the Gate enforce?

It checks identity, authority, provenance, consistency, solvency, compliance, and execution constraints against the declared mandate.

Does Vishwa hold funds or private keys?

No. Vishwa governs execution without taking custody of customer funds or private keys.

How is sensitive state protected?

Context is minimized, protected during coordination, and disclosed only as permitted.
